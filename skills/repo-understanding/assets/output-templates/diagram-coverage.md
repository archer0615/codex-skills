# Diagram Coverage

| Diagram type | Scope | 涵蓋的證據 | Artifact | 狀態 | 驗證 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <architecture / workflow / sequence / dataflow / lifecycle> | <independent-scope> | <evidence> | <path-or-none> | <CURRENT / BLOCKED / NOT APPLICABLE> | <semantic-and-render-check> |

同一 diagram type 的獨立責任邊界各自佔一列；不要以單張巨型圖取代 scope coverage。
